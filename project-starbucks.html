Who did the offer actually persuade?

Sending an offer to someone who would have bought anyway is a waste. Uplift modelling tries to find the people an offer genuinely changes.

In progress2026SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI

The question

Which customers does an offer actually persuade, and which would have bought anyway?

What I did

  • Starting from the Udacity Starbucks capstone dataset.
  • Sorting customers into persuadables, sure things, lost causes and sleeping dogs.
  • Comparing a two-model approach: one model for customers who got the offer, one for those who did not.
  • Adding an Excel cost calculator that turns uplift into money, then a Power BI or Tableau dashboard.

What I found

  • Still in progress, so there are no headline numbers yet. I will add results here once the model has been properly validated.

Where it stands

Uses the public Udacity Starbucks capstone dataset. Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Starbucks.
