Customer churn model

Predicting who will leave a bank is a ranking problem more than a prediction problem. This project treats it that way, and is honest about how much the model can say.

Completed2026BigQuery SQL, Random Forest, Tableau
  • 37%churn rate in the 1K customer records
  • 52.6%of feature importance sits in age
  • 0.60ROC-AUC, honest and modest

The question

Which customers are most at risk of churning, and how much can a model genuinely tell us?

What I did

  • Prepared the customer data with BigQuery SQL.
  • Trained a Random Forest classifier to estimate churn risk.
  • Built Tableau views to show risk across customer groups.
  • Packaged the results as LinkedIn carousel assets.

What I found

  • ROC-AUC landed between 0.576 and 0.601 across runs.
  • Age alone carried 52.6% of feature importance, and age plus income carried about 74% of the signal.
  • That is only a little better than chance, so the model is useful for ranking risk, not for confident calls on a single customer.

Why I show it anyway

I would rather show a modest result clearly than dress it up. The sensible next step is better features and a cost-based view of which customers are worth contacting.
